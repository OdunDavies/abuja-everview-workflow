Workflow spec — Everview Properties

Prototype logic only. Next connect: WhatsApp Business API, Google Sheets or Airtable, info@everviewproperties.ng, Meta lead ads.

1. Trigger

Website form, IG DM keyword, WhatsApp first message, or missed call after hours.

2. Score

Budget + district + timeline + buy/invest. Hot ≥ 75, Warm ≥ 50, else Cold.

3. Instant reply

FAQ pack: public price band, 1–4 month plan language from their site, inspection options, AGIS process — no fake C of O claims.

4. Assign

Hot → closer on duty. Warm → nurture. Cold → Saturday broadcast list.

Nurture sequence (copy)

  1. Instant: Received. {district} plots that fit {budget} — reply 1 for price list, 2 for Saturday inspection, 3 for video tour.
  2. +4 hours if no reply: Still want Lakeview / Fortress / Pride Land? Slot left on Saturday 10:00 WAT.
  3. +48 hours: Payment plan is the usual 1–4 months Everview already advertises. Abroad buyers get documents pack + video first.

Inspection reminders

T-24h: date, meeting point, what to bring (ID). T-2h: driver/agent name + pin.

Weekly digest

Monday 8:00 WAT to sales lead: Hot waiting, no-shows, unassigned WhatsApp threads older than 12 hours.
